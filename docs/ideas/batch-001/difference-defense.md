## Candidate-specific defense

### I01 — Current/history joint feasibility

**Honest scope:** conditional theory/boundary and diagnosis; the pseudoinverse/nullspace construction is an attributed mathematical baseline, not a new optimizer. OGD/OGD+ already uses protected output Jacobians and residual-dependent projected update costs (G S03–S04); AlphaEdit and NB-LoRA supply strong geometric alternatives (R §6; G S01).

**Necessary distinguishing prediction:** paired current/history queries with the same contrast-output definition must expose a consequential incompatibility or target-aligned small singular direction that ordinary activation overlap or gradient cosine misses. Only singular directions with nonzero target component increase the required local step. The claimed endpoint is local feasibility/step cost; increased historical harm additionally requires curvature and cannot be inferred from singular values alone.

**Removes the need:** ordinary time conditioning or same-information text retrieval resolves the current/history conflict at the required cost; existing projected-feature analysis already supplies the same actionable certificate; or the local certificate adds no reliable decision beyond those alternatives. A useful theory contribution would still need a strict, consequential comparison with the closest theorem rather than this standard specialization alone.

### I02 — Separate obsolete, replacement, and remaining mass

**Honest scope:** attributed baseline. CUD Appendix A directly gives minimum-KL projection with wrong-class upper mass, correct-neighborhood lower mass, exponential multipliers, and preserved unconstrained ratios (T, CUD Eqs.2/11). A temporal application would need an independent, native task consequence caused by validity-conditioned event semantics, with the same labels and event information available to CUD and positive-SFT/forget/retain baselines. Full-sequence events are not automatically computable from token mass.

**Removes the need:** CUD instantiated with O=wrong and N=replacement, or a simple explicit replacement objective, achieves the same obsolete suppression, acquisition, and U/history behavior at equal information and cost. Do not admit an algorithm on a more elaborate event naming scheme.

**Removes the need:** the paired roles are not functionally separable, retirement releases no useful target component, existing temporal methods already implement the same transition, or ordinary time replay/retrieval solves it within the resource target. ### I14 — Fixed-base sequence retention versus student-prefix training

**Honest scope:** conditional diagnosis/measurement and estimator boundary; no novel importance-weighting formula is established. **Necessary distinguishing prediction:** a consequential old capability must depend on base-likely branches underrepresented in student rollouts; correcting the *retention prefix measure* must preferentially repair that failure at matched acquisition and total cost. **Removes the need:** equal-budget base replay already supplies adequate prefix coverage, the base-only branches are irrelevant to native outcomes, or an existing off-policy method estimates the same frozen-base objective with equivalent information and cost. Changing the objective from IW-OPD does not by itself justify a new estimator or prove a native effect.

### I21 — Source-count and within-source noise at a fixed SFT objective

**Honest scope:** conditional native diagnosis/measurement; the law-of-total-covariance and Neyman allocation remain attributed baselines. No new allocation algorithm is defended. This is a distinct hypothesis from I02 and cannot inherit its math/source/admission review. It shares I11's local quadratic object but asks about stochastic rather than mean-step causes.

**Necessary distinguishing prediction:** conditional on the same current parameters and fixed mixture weights, consequential old-distribution KL drift must remain when `μᵀF_Vμ` is small, and vary with the measured `tr(F_VΣ)` of the *actual* step distribution. Source reweighting that changes μ is not this comparison. VR-MCL's meta-hypergradient momentum variance reduction is a neighbor; its inspected mechanism is not this fixed-mixture source-count decomposition. IDEAL/DynamixSFT mixture-objective changes also do not isolate it.

For B IID mixture examples, covariance is `Σ_mix/B`; a different sampler, shared source per batch, order dependence, finite-population dependence, or Adam state requires its actual covariance. Proportional integer stratification can remove the random-source-count term when feasible. Neyman gains require the stated continuous/equal-cost assumptions or their cost-aware constrained version; positive per-source counts, integer rounding, small B, and unequal token/FLOP costs can reverse the comparison. A uniform local remainder over all realized steps, or separate tail control, is needed. Same fixed target does not mean the entire stochastic training trajectories have identical realized mean paths.

**Removes the need:** the trace term is negligible, mean/representation/optimizer effects explain native harm, ordinary larger batch/lower learning rate/standard stratification already closes the gap, or estimating the protected Fisher consumes unavailable old information or eliminates the resource advantage. Exact whole-step protection in `ker F_V` eliminates this term; do not claim unavoidable noise harm for exact NB-LoRA/GPM conditions. This scalar local-KL expectation is not automatically a native correctness or long-run forgetting theorem.
