## Six frozen fields

### 1. `natural_failure`

On the released Landmark row stream, Algorithm 4 refreshes when its energy
trigger `E_t >= c * E_last_refresh` fires; this trigger is not itself an
accuracy-failure test.  At each such natural event, score the pre-refresh
projector and retain an eligible failed episode only when that projector violates
the candidate-independent accuracy envelope defined below.  Near a dense
spectral cutoff, many projectors can have similar reconstruction loss while the
exact optimizer can be far from the current projector.  The unresolved
real-system failure is therefore:

> a post-warmup refresh incurs a consequential projector movement even though
> the strongest frozen simple alternatives cannot simultaneously preserve the
> declared reconstruction envelope and reduce that movement.

The accepted single-event constructions and gap-weighted potential motivate
this question but do not establish its frequency on Landmark.  Stage A proves
selected-prefix identities and update-cost coverage only; it supplies no
all-prefix failure census.

### 2. `population_id`, `population`

- `population_id`: `landmark-first5000-rank25-accuracy-failed-refreshes-v1`.
- Natural event frame: all post-warmup energy-trigger refreshes generated by the six frozen
  Algorithm-4 arms (`c in {1.1,2,2.5,5,10,100}`) on rows 1--5,000 of the exact
  author `landmark.mtx`, released order, rank 25, no scaling, with the reviewed
  259-column active map and ambient zero embedding.
- Eligible failure population: exactly those natural events for which the
  pre-refresh projector violates the frozen accuracy envelope below.  Ordinary
  energy refreshes whose old projector still meets the envelope are retained as
  unaffected frame members but are outside the conditional failure denominator.
  Events with invalid or unavailable native scoring are retained as missing and
  make the screen incomplete; they are never silently excluded as successes.
- Warmup transitions through prefix 25 are excluded from the primary population
  and retained separately as initialization diagnostics.
- Events are not independent repetitions.  The denominator is the exact number
  of eligible refresh events after valid all-prefix scoring, not 5,000 prefixes,
  65,000 update records or 13 arms.

### 3. `prevalence`

For each event `e=(c,t)`, let `P^-_e` be that Algorithm-4 arm's actual projector
immediately before its energy-triggered refresh, let `P^*_e` be the fresh exact
rank-25 SVD projector at the same prefix, let `E_t=||A_{1:t}||_F^2`, and let
`OPT_t=L_t(P^*_e)`.  Freeze the candidate-independent additive envelope

\[
B_t=OPT_t+0.01E_t.
\]

The one-percent normalized-additive allowance is applied at the event only.  It
is distinct from the scorer's numerical tolerance and from any candidate's
`rho_lo`/`rho_hi`.  It remains defined at near-zero OPT; every event uses the
reviewed direct-SVD/direct-residual fallback before classification.  A natural
event is an eligible failed episode iff `L_t(P^-_e)>B_t` after those fallbacks.

For every frozen simple control `a`, let `Q_{a,t}` be its actual output at the
same prefix and define the event-anchored movement

\[
d_e(Q_{a,t})=\|Q_{a,t}-P^-_e\|_F^2,
\qquad d^*_e=\|P^*_e-P^-_e\|_F^2.
\]

The control solves the event only if

\[
L_t(Q_{a,t})\le B_t
\quad\text{and}\quad
d_e(Q_{a,t})\le d^*_e-0.10(2k).
\]

This deliberately compares every output to the target arm's same pre-refresh
projector.  It does not use the control's own adjacent transition and does not
construct a switchable control trajectory.  The event-wise oracle is only a
candidate-independent diagnostic of whether the frozen simple family contains a
qualifying output at that event.

The estimand is

\[
\Pr(\text{affected refresh}\mid\text{eligible accuracy-failed refresh episode})
=N_{\rm affected}/N_{\rm eligible}.
\]

An eligible failed episode is `affected` only when `d^*_e >= 0.10(2k)` and
no frozen simple control solves it by the exact rule above.  Report the complete
natural event frame, eligible denominator, missing classifications, arm/event
dependence and exact `(c,t)` identities; parameter arms and adjacent prefixes do
not manufacture independent repetitions or an i.i.d. confidence interval.

The rate is currently **unknown**.  Stage A's 35-prefix identities and full
update timings do not contain the all-prefix loss/OPT/fallback results needed to
classify events.

### 4. `consequence`

Primary endpoint: steady cumulative squared-projector Frobenius recourse, under
the frozen reconstruction envelope and with every per-prefix loss, OPT and
near-zero fallback valid.  Per-event normalized movement is

\[
r_t/(2k)=\|P_t-P_{t-1}\|_F^2/(2k)\in[0,1].
\]

The parent matters only if affected events contribute a nontrivial fraction of
steady recourse while simple alternatives fail the joint accuracy condition.
Runtime is a required secondary endpoint, separated into update, reference,
scoring, serialization and pipeline CPU/wall/RSS; CPU fullness is not the
scientific objective.

### 5. `strong_simple_alternative`

The strongest simple alternative is the event-wise diagnostic oracle over the already frozen
non-candidate controls: keep/fixed projector, periodic refresh at 10 or 100,
fresh exact SVD, and qualified Frequent Directions at `ell=50` or `ell=100`.
It marks `alternative_solves=true` only when at least one actual control output
meets `L_t(Q)<=B_t` and the event-anchored movement reduction of `0.10(2k)`.
It receives no future rows and cannot use candidate outputs.  Selecting different
controls at different events is not a realizable cumulative path, so this oracle
can qualify the parent census but cannot be reported as a trajectory baseline or
cumulative-recourse result.

`simple_alternatives`:

1. keep the current projector at the event;
2. fixed-after-warmup projector;
3. periodic refresh, intervals 10 and 100;
4. fresh exact rank-25 SVD;
5. qualified FD, `ell=50` and `ell=100`.

The frozen author `ell+1` FD is a source diagnostic and cannot satisfy the
strong-alternative role.

### 6. `falsifiable_prediction`

Natural Gate 0 passes only if the independently scored event census contains at
least 20 valid eligible failed episodes, at least 5 affected episodes, affected prevalence at
least 0.10, mean normalized movement among affected events at least 0.10, and
at least half of eligible failed episodes remain unresolved by the event-wise
simple oracle.  Fewer than 20 valid eligible episodes, missing scorer coverage or
an incomplete event frame yields `INCONCLUSIVE`.  With adequate complete valid
coverage, falling below any substantive affected-count/prevalence/movement/
unresolved threshold yields `KILL` for this Parent Problem as a supported
Landmark method target; neither outcome falsifies the earlier theorem audit.

If the parent passes, a successful selected method must lower steady cumulative
recourse at matched valid reconstruction loss and must not merely defer movement
to later prefixes.  If benefit is not concentrated at dense-cutoff/small-loss-
excess events, the current spectral explanation is rejected even if aggregate
recourse changes.

## Frozen Gate-0 thresholds

```yaml
gate_0_thresholds:
  min_cases: 20
  min_affected: 5
  min_prevalence: 0.10
  min_mean_loss: 0.10
  min_unresolved_fraction: 0.50
```

`min_mean_loss` is the mean normalized exact-refresh movement `d^*_e/(2k)` among
affected events.  The 0.10 thresholds require at least ten percent of the
maximum possible rank-k projector movement, avoiding classification by numerical
noise.  Twenty cases and five affected cases are minimal discovery screens, not
population inference or confirmation.  The unresolved-fraction threshold demands
that the strongest simple family fail on a majority of eligible failed episodes before a
new mechanism is worth the required implementation investment.
